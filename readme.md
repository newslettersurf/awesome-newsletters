# Awesome Newsletters [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated, GitHub-style list of the best email newsletters to subscribe to — 445+ newsletters across 60 categories and 45 professions.

This is a directory of widely read newsletters covering technology, business, marketing, personal finance, health, science, and dozens of other topics. Each entry links to the newsletter's own website so you can subscribe directly, plus a `review` link to its full listing on [Newsletter Surf](https://newsletter.surf), a searchable newsletter directory with categories, saved lists, and reader reviews. The [Professions](#professions) section further breaks newsletters down by job function — software engineers, marketers, lawyers, doctors, and more — for readers looking for newsletters relevant to their field rather than a general topic.

Use the [Contents](#contents) below to jump to a category, or browse by [profession](#professions) if you're looking for newsletters tailored to your job.

Inspired by [awesome](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome).
